1Getting started

Attendance replaces the paper register. A teacher marks a class in a few taps. A student sees exactly where they stand. The head of department (HOD) sees every section, and every change is written to an audit trail.

Who uses it

RoleWhat they do
StudentChecks their attendance, applies for leave with a certificate, joins events that count as attendance.
TeacherMarks the register for their own classes, corrects mistakes within 48 hours, cancels a class they cannot take, decides leave for the section they are class teacher of.
HODBuilds courses, sections, subjects and timetables, adds students, cancels classes for holidays and events, reads every record.
MentorRuns clubs and posts events that can credit class attendance.
AdministratorCreates the HOD, teacher and mentor accounts and resets passwords. Does not teach.

Opening the app

Scan the QR code on the notice board, a slide or the stall with your phone camera. It opens the introduction page, which anyone can read before they have an account. From there, tap Sign in.

Keep it on your home screenOn Android, open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.

Signing in

  1. Your department creates your account and gives you a login ID and a first password in person. Nothing is emailed.
  2. Type the login ID and password, and tap Sign in.
  3. Open Profile and change the password. Whoever created your account knows the first one.

The left rail (the bottom bar on a phone) lists only what your role can use. Guide repeats the steps for your role inside the app, and has a button to download this manual.

2How attendance is counted

Your percentage is measured against the classes your section actually held. A class counts as held once its teacher takes the register.

Attendance % = present ÷ (present + absent + not marked)
SituationHow it counts
PresentCounts for you.
AbsentCounts against you.
Not markedThe class was held but the register has no mark for you. It counts as missed; if you were there, ask your teacher to correct it.
Approved leaveLeft out completely, so approved leave never lowers your percentage.
Cancelled classNever held, so it counts for nobody and nothing can be marked.
Event creditA period credited by an event you took part in counts as present.
Before you joinedClasses held before your account existed do not count against you.

Example. Your section held 91 classes. You were present at 88 and absent from 3: 88 ÷ 91 = 96.7%.

The minimum, and how to reach it

Each course has a minimum percentage set by the HOD; 75% if none was set. When you are below it, the app tells you how many classes you must attend in a row to reach it. It never tells you how many you can afford to skip.

The 90% badge

★ 90%+ attendanceReach 90% overall and a gold badge appears on your overview and your profile, and a star appears next to your name in the register your teachers read. Below 90%, the app tells you how many classes in a row it takes to earn it.

3For students

Your overview

The first page after signing in. The ring is your percentage against the minimum for your course. Below it: how many classes it takes to reach the minimum, the badge, today's classes (cancelled ones are struck through with the reason) and any classes cancelled in the days ahead.

Analytics

Analytics breaks your attendance down by subject: classes held, present, absent, not marked, leave, percentage, and classes needed. Attendance from events lists every class an event credited for you.

Your timetable

Schedule shows your section's week, with period times, the break and the after-hours activities, exactly as on the printed routine.

Applying for leave

  1. Open Leave desk.
  2. Pick the From and To dates and write the reason.
  3. Attach the medical certificate with Take a photo or Choose a file (JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC or PDF, under 2 MB).
  4. Tap Send request. Your class teacher and the HOD are told.

Once approved, every class in those days is marked as leave, and leave does not count against you.

Events that count as attendance

An event can credit class periods: if you take part, you are marked present in whatever class your section has in those periods. Tap Ask to join. When the organiser approves you, or adds your attendance directly, the event card shows exactly what it did.

4For teachers

Your day

Your overview lists today's classes with their times. A class you cannot take can be cancelled right there with one tap on cancel, and undone the same way. Your students and the HOD are told.

Marking the register

  1. Open Attendance.
  2. Pick the Course, Section, Date and Period. The roster loads from the timetable, so you can only open your own classes.
  3. Tap Mark all present, then tap A for each absentee. L is leave.
  4. Check the tally at the bottom, and tap Save attendance.
Mark everyone before savingA student left unmarked in a class you saved counts as having missed it. The tally warns you when someone is still unmarked.

A row tinted purple was credited through an event, and says so; saving the register again keeps it credited.

Correcting a mark

Tap Correct on the student's row, choose the new status, give a short reason, and tap Save correction. You can correct your own marks for 48 hours. After that, the HOD makes the correction, and it is recorded against their name.

Cancelling a class

From your overview, tap cancel next to the class. Or, on the marking screen, open the class and tap Not taking this class? Cancel it instead, add a reason if you like, and tap Cancel this class. A cancelled class cannot be marked and counts for nobody. Restore this class undoes it. You can only cancel your own classes, and never one whose register you already took.

Leave for your section

If you are a class teacher, requests from your section arrive on the Leave desk. Open the certificate with View medical certificate, add a note if you like, and tap Approve or Reject. Approving marks leave across every class of those days. Change this decision reverses it later, with a reason.

Records and exports

Records shows attendance by student, with anyone under the minimum flagged in red. Export all and Export under minimum download a CSV that opens in Excel. On a phone, they open the share sheet, so the file can go straight to WhatsApp or email.

5For the head of department

Setting up the department

Nobody can mark a register until the timetable exists, and each step depends on the one before it. Your overview shows a checklist until the department is set up.

#WhereWhat
1SetupAdd the course (for example BBA).
2SetupAdd its sections (D1, D2…) and subjects.
3SetupCreate teacher accounts, or ask the administrator to.
4Setup → AllocationGive each section a class teacher. Leave requests go to them.
5ScheduleSet the week, then fill each section's timetable (see below).
6SetupSet the minimum attendance percentage for the course.
7Setup → AccountsAdd students one at a time, or paste a CSV with Bulk import: roll_number,name,login_id,password, one per line.

Building the timetable

  1. Open Schedule and pick the course. Every section gets its own grid, even one with nothing in it yet.
  2. Set Days a week (for example Monday–Friday) and Periods a day, then tap Save week.
  3. Optionally, tap Timings to print the period times, the break and what happens after classes on the grid, exactly as on the notice board.
  4. Tap add in any empty period, choose the Subject and Teacher, and tap Save period.
  5. Use change to give a period another subject or teacher, and clear to empty it.
The builder keeps the timetable consistent
  • A teacher cannot be in two sections at the same time. The teacher list says who is already teaching another section in that period.
  • The week cannot be shortened while a class still sits in a day or period that would disappear. Clear it first.
  • Clearing a period does not touch registers already taken in it.

Loading a printed routine in one go

A whole routine can be loaded from a file instead of being typed period by period. The D2 BBA routine ships with the app as server/src/infra/db/timetables/d2-bba.json, and whoever runs the deployment can load it:

DATABASE_URL="…" npm run timetable:import -- server/src/infra/db/timetables/d2-bba.json --dry-run
DATABASE_URL="…" npm run timetable:import -- server/src/infra/db/timetables/d2-bba.json

It creates the course, section and subjects if needed, sets the week and timings, and makes the section's timetable match the file. A teacher it does not know yet gets an account with a one-time password, printed once, to hand over in person. Before importing into a real college's database, put each teacher's full name, and a loginId if you like, in the file. A class the routine lists without a teacher, such as Wednesday's review session, is left for you to allocate from Schedule.

Cancelling classes in one tap

For a holiday, a strike, exam day or a fest: open Schedule, tap Cancel classes, pick the date and tap the red button. That cancels every class in the college for the day. You can narrow it to one course or one section, pick periods, and give a reason, which students see.

When classes are cancelled:

  • students of the section and the teachers of those classes are notified;
  • the classes cannot be marked, and count towards nobody's attendance;
  • approved leave and event credits skip them;
  • a class whose register was already taken is left alone and reported: it happened.

For a college-wide event, open it under Events and tap Cancel … for this event: its credit periods, or the whole day, are cancelled in one tap, with the event named as the reason.

Your overview

The overview counts the classes held so far, the classes held today and the cancellations ahead. It also shows average attendance by section, leave waiting for a decision and the latest activity.

Records

Pick a course or section, optionally a date range. Each row shows classes held, present, absent, not marked, leave and the percentage. Rows under the minimum are flagged; a ★ marks the 90% badge. Above the table, the number of classes the section held in that range. Print / PDF uses the browser's own PDF printing.

Leave, analytics and the audit trail

The Leave desk works as for a class teacher, for every section. Analytics shows each section's average against the classes it held, and how many were cancelled. Activities is the full audit trail: every mark, correction, cancellation, approval, credit and export, with who did it and why.

6For mentors

Clubs

Under Clubs, create your club and add members by their login ID.

Events

  1. Under Events, fill in Post an event: title, date, and who can see it: all students, or your club's members only.
  2. If the event replaces classes, tap the periods it counts for under Periods this event counts for.
  3. Tap Post event.

Giving students their attendance

Students ask to join; tap Review join requests and Approve. Or record who took part directly: tap Add attendance, paste their login IDs (or tap Everyone who asked or All … club members), and tap the button. Their periods are credited at once, their teachers are told, and the table shows what was credited for each student.

Why a period might not be creditedThe student's section has no class then, the class was cancelled, or the event is on a Sunday. The result says which.

7For administrators

On a brand new deployment, the first person to open the site creates the administrator account. After that, every account is created inside the app.

  1. Under Accounts, create the HOD account and hand over the login ID and password in person.
  2. Create a second administrator, so one lost password is never the end.
  3. Use Reset password when someone is locked out; it clears the lock immediately.

Sharing the app with a QR code

Under Guide, Share the app shows a QR code that opens the introduction page. Print a poster prints it large, on its own page, for a notice board or a stall.

8Passwords, privacy and the audit trail

If you are a…Ask this person to reset your password
StudentYour class teacher, or the HOD
Teacher, mentor or HODAn administrator
AdministratorAnother administrator
  • There is no reset by email anywhere. Credentials are handed over in person.
  • Repeated wrong passwords lock an account for a few minutes. A reset clears the lock.
  • You are notified whenever someone else changes your password. If you did not expect it, say so.
  • Medical certificates are never public links: only the student, their class teacher and the HOD can open one, and each view is recorded.
  • Every correction, override, cancellation, approval, credit and export is written to the audit trail, with who did it and why.

9Running the tech fest demo

A demonstration deployment has a sample college and one-tap demo accounts, so visitors can try every role without typing a password. The demo college is built around the real D2 BBA routine; its students, marks and events are made up.

Setting it up (once)

  1. Use a separate, empty database. Never the college's real one.
  2. Create the tables, then the demo college: 
    DATABASE_URL="…" npm run migrate
DATABASE_URL="…" npm run seed:demo
  3. Deploy with the environment variable DEMO_MODE=1. The sign-in and introduction pages then show the demo buttons.
  4. Sign in as the administrator, open Guide, and print the QR poster for the stall.

The demo accounts

Every demo account's password is demo1234. Visitors cannot change it, reset another demo account's password, or remove a demo account, so nobody locks the next visitor out.

A three-minute walkthrough

  1. Scan the QR poster: the introduction page opens.
  2. Teacher: open today's class, tap Mark all present, tap two absentees, save. Two taps plus one per absentee.
  3. Student: the ring, the gold badge, and the classes the B-Plan contest credited.
  4. Student at risk: the red ring, and how many classes in a row it takes to recover.
  5. HOD: the real D2 BBA routine on Schedule. Cancel a day's classes in one tap, then undo it. Records, with flags and stars.
  6. Mentor: approve the Case Study Challenge requests; each student's credited period appears.

Between sessions

Visitors change things. To put the college back as it was:

DATABASE_URL="…" npm run seed:demo -- --reset

It only wipes a database that seed:demo created itself, and refuses any other. The history is dated back from the day it runs, so reset on the morning of the fest.

10Questions and fixes

What you seeWhat it means, and what to do
"The timetable has no class in that period for this section"That period is empty on the timetable. The HOD fills it from Schedule.
"This class was cancelled…"It was called off in advance. Whoever cancelled it, or the HOD, can restore it from Schedule.
"That period belongs to another teacher"You can only mark classes the timetable gives you.
"The 48-hour edit window has closed"Ask the HOD to correct it; the change is recorded with their name.
A class shows as not markedIt was held but you have no mark. If you were there, ask the teacher to correct the register.
An event did not change my percentageOpen the event card: it says which periods were credited and why any were not.
"That teacher already has a class in this period"The teacher is in another section then. Pick another teacher or period.
"Too many failed attempts"Wait a few minutes, or ask for a password reset, which clears the lock at once.
My percentage went down although nothing changedA class you were not marked for was held. Check Not marked under Analytics.

11Words used in the app

SectionA class group within a course, such as D2. Students, timetables and class teachers belong to a section.
PeriodOne slot in the day, such as period 1, 10:15–11:15.
RegisterThe attendance list for one class on one date.
Held classA class whose register was taken. Attendance is counted against held classes.
Not markedA held class with no mark for a student. Counts as missed.
Cancelled classA class called off in advance. Never marked, never counted.
Approved leaveLeave a class teacher or the HOD approved. Left out of the percentage.
Event creditA period marked present because the student took part in an event.
MinimumThe attendance percentage a course requires; 75% unless the HOD set another.
BadgeThe gold 90%+ attendance recognition.
Class teacherThe teacher responsible for a section, who decides its leave requests.
